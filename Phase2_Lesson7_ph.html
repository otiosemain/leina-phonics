Story: Phone and Photo

Mum took a nice photo of the big elephant at the zoo.

She showed it to me on her phone later that day.

We also saw a happy dolphin swimming in the pool.

I wrote the word in my alphabet book when we got home.

Practice: Point to the coloured “ph” and say the /f/ sound, then blend the whole word.
