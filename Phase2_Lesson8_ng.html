Story: Sing a Song

The king liked to sing a long song every morning.

He wore a big gold ring on his finger.

The song was so pretty that everyone stopped to listen.

Then there was a loud bang from the kitchen door.

Practice: Point to the coloured “ng” and say the sound (like the end of “sing”), then blend the whole word.
