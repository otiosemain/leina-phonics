Story: A Sunny Day

We ate cake under the big tree.

It started to rain but then the sun came out.

We rowed the small boat on the blue lake.

Everyone was very happy at the end of the day.

Red marks the long vowel or vowel team. Other colours change so neighbouring parts look different.
