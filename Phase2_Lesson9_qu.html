Story: The Quick Queen

The queen was very quick to answer the quiz.

Everyone stayed quiet while she thought.

She did not quit until she finished every question.

Then she drew a big square on the board.

Practice: Point to the coloured “qu” and say the /kw/ sound, then blend the whole word.
